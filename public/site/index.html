Questions, answered.

Is this real iMessage?+

Yes. Every message is sent from a real iPhone number through Apple's Messages app, so it arrives as a native iMessage with read receipts and typing indicators.

Does it work with our CRM?+

Triggers come from your CRM, forms or scheduling tool through Zapier, Make or our API. Common setups fire on a demo request, a booked meeting and a no-show.

What happens with Android users?+

They get a regular text from the same number, so no lead falls through the cracks.

Are there sending limits?+

Messages are unlimited. New conversations are paced per number, about 50 a day, to keep each number healthy. More reps, more numbers.

Can we cold blast a purchased list?+

No. B2Blue is built for one-to-one conversations with leads who opted in, like demo requests and booked meetings. That's what keeps reply rates high and numbers healthy.
