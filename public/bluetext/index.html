Questions

Is this real iMessage?+

Yes. Every message is sent from a real iPhone number through Apple's Messages app, so it arrives as a native blue bubble with read receipts and typing indicators.

What happens with Android users?+

They get a regular text from the same number, so nobody falls through the cracks.

Are there sending limits?+

Messages are unlimited. New conversations are paced per number, about 50 a day, to keep each number healthy. Need more volume? Add numbers for more reps.

Can I blast my whole list?+

No. Blue Text is built for one-to-one conversations with opted-in leads. That's what keeps reply rates high and lines alive.
